What this does and does not settle.

Settled: the cheap route is closed

A frameshift proxy derived from the 20bp protospacer carries no usable signal here. Building a hit-caller on it would be building on noise.

Not settled: a real repair model

inDelphi and FORECasT read ~60bp of genomic context around the cut. GeCKOv2 stores no cut position, so that sequence does not exist in this database and was never tested.

Unruled-out: position

Guides in early exons deplete more regardless of repair. Without cut positions this could not be controlled, so even a positive result would have needed a second look.

Already solved elsewhere

JACKS and MAGeCK-NEST already downweight weak guides by watching what they actually do. An empirical measurement beats a prediction of the same thing.
