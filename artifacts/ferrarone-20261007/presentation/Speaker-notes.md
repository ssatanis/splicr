## 2. The paper’s biological question

Ferrarone et al., PNAS 2024, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2403685121. Full title: Genome-wide CRISPR screens in spheroid culture reveal that the tumor suppressor LKB1 inhibits growth via the PIKFYVE lipid kinase. LKB1 is a tumor suppressor. In this study restoring LKB1 restrains growth more strongly in spheroid culture. The screen and follow-up experiments implicate the PIKFYVE complex and growth factor receptor trafficking. The image is the authors' complete, unmodified Figure 4, used here to discuss their experiments. Red fluorescence reports internalized EGF, not a SplicR prediction. ## 10. A completed report exposes uncertainty

Actual production full RRA+MLE report for spheroid WT versus plasmid. The primary candidate count uses RRA, with MLE statistics retained separately. 18,049 genes have recorded statistics. 178 pass the SplicR two-direction FDR threshold 0.05, of which 172 have at least one artifact flag. Flags prompt inspection and do not automatically disprove a gene. Of these 178 RRA candidates, 176 are depleted and 2 enriched. 154 carry the stored DepMap pan-essential flag, consistent with many general survival dependencies in a plasmid comparison. Another 16 carry an Atlas recurring-hit flag. Flag categories overlap: 170 frequent hitters, 58 promiscuous-guide flags and 44 multi-site-guide flags. Their sum is not a count of distinct genes. Common dependency annotations are context, not proof of the mechanism in this experiment. The WT-versus-EV endpoint comparison addresses relative abundance under LKB1 restoration and must be interpreted with its own quality and statistical limits. QC warns about replicate disagreement and count skew. No gene has recorded bench validation in this workspace. Numerical completion is not biological validation. All six completed RRA reports passed live API and browser export checks.

Values rounded only for display. None passes the declared RRA or MLE FDR threshold 0.05 here. Spheroid EV-versus-plasmid MLE beta values are FIG4 1.1152, VAC14 -0.031009 and PIKFYVE 0.37959. VAC14 meets both published WT-enriched/EV-low beta thresholds, but FIG4 does not meet their EV-low cutoff in this separately fitted design. The paper’s methods use WT beta >0.5 / EV <0.5 for pathway analysis; its Figure 3 caption uses >1 / <1. Both cutoff passages describe pathway-analysis input for Figure 3D, not FDR significance criteria for the entire screen. The actual implemented rule is not established by these descriptions. Ask which rule generated Figure 3D without assuming a typo or treating the difference as evidence against the biological conclusions. Original normalization, guide filtering and the design also need investigator clarification. The authors used MLE beta nomination and wet-lab follow-up; beta criteria and FDR calls are different evidence criteria. PIKFYVE guides were not enriched in WT spheroids in the paper, and pharmacological/rescue experiments established its role. These results do not establish exact reproduction or superior biology. ## 15. SplicR alongside Broad Apron

Primary sources checked 2026-10-07: Broad's post-screen page https://portals.broadinstitute.org/gpp/public/resources/post-screen and Apron guide https://docs.google.com/document/d/14ry2-S3zWCwJVZzxS2QLSdNGqyl6QaFK7rhkcyh7jXU/preview. Apron is a guided web app with QC, replicate exploration, plots and downloadable gene lists. It is optimized for PoolQ counts with .chip annotations and supports other formatted inputs. Its documented target aggregation uses Stouffer z-scores and an empirical null from control constructs. Its guide supports manual reference and replicate definitions and multiple data configurations. SplicR's tested Ferrarone flow adds exact source identification, guide-ID annotation repair and automatic six-comparison suggestions. SplicR uses MAGeCK RRA plus optional MLE, preserving separate statistics. These are workflow differences, not an experimentally measured accuracy or usability victory. We did not run Apron on these files or benchmark its runtime. The Broad GPP page documents CRISPRko/a/i with Cas9/Cas12a for single-gene constructs, broader than the Cas9 knockout evidence in this particular demo.

## 16. Other tools and SplicR’s role

Sources: official MAGeCK documentation https://sourceforge.net/p/mageck/wiki/usage/ and the original PinAPL-Py paper https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-16193-9. MAGeCK offers mature RRA/MLE analysis and explicit design matrices via command line. PinAPL-Py already offers a web interface, automated adapter-based guide extraction, custom libraries, QC and adjustable ranking. The comparison describes those published capabilities and does not establish that their hosted services are currently operational. SplicR's implemented role is a shared research workflow around established statistical methods with source recognition, reviewable configuration, guide-level evidence and saved receipts. Interface convenience, model prediction, statistical significance and biological validation are different dimensions. No head-to-head performance or accuracy ranking was performed.

## 19. Methods and scientific limits

Sources: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2403685121, https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/8DEPIT, https://sourceforge.net/p/mageck/wiki/usage/. Reanalysis uses deposited count tables, exact TKOv3 guide-ID annotation, MAGeCK 0.5.9.5, median normalization, RRA union FDR threshold 0.05. It retains all deposited guide rows and does not recreate the authors' Cutadapt/Bowtie processing or unspecified implementation of the read-count <30 filter. Authors' methods describe that filter but not which samples define it. MLE runs use explicit baseline-first design, seed 0, 10 permutation rounds and grouped permutations. We did not reduce permutation rounds to claim a faster run. Copy-number correction, Chronos, BAGEL2 and DrugZ are off in this study's completed RRA cohort. Core essential separation supports count-table fitness QC, but replicate disagreement and skew remain warnings. Count tables cannot establish sequencing mapping efficiency. Atlas uses previous-screen evidence to describe recurring hits. That context does not independently validate this study's candidates. The saved receipt records a publication exclusion policy for benchmark holdouts, but it does not establish that this specific paper was held out. The validation network has no fitted outcomes cohort on this deployed worker and refuses probabilities with a reason. Missing predictions and absent methods must stay unavailable. Separate WT/EV culture tests do not establish a culture interaction.
